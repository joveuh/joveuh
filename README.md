### Jove Kane

Forward-deployed AI engineer. Day job: operating systems and full-stack engineering.

I build the plumbing that keeps LLM output usable when the model underneath
isn't reliable — a swappable judge for when one turns out miscalibrated, a
parser that survives a model that doesn't answer in clean JSON, a fallback
for when a call just fails.

🔭 Currently building: a monthly loop that turns one paper into a small
runnable artifact and a write-up.

**Pinned below:**

- **[human-atlas](https://github.com/joveuh/human-atlas)** — a browser atlas
  of the adult human body. Loads 2,234 modelled anatomical pieces from the
  open BodyParts3D dataset, groups them into 15 toggleable body systems, and
  lets you explode the body into a sorted inventory or search for any single
  piece.
- **[paper-shelf](https://github.com/joveuh/paper-shelf)** — a weekly arXiv
  sweep, LLM-judged and ranked into a reading shelf, built around one
  finding: a cheap local judge is consistent but not honest, so the judge
  has to be swappable.
